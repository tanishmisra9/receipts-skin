    Limits

    • Three faces and 12 invented products. Small samples, so read the counts, not the percentages.
    • The noise margins come from one face (three ordinary photo changes). Four other faces are a test, and the band flagged only some of the flips there.
    • Test D and Test E are two more portraits used only for this test and never shown, by Divine Effiong and Luke Southern on Unsplash. Both are dark-background, dramatically lit photos, which is a harder case than a selfie by a window.
    • Five more test portraits (Test F to J, never shown) were scanned once and once darker, and nothing else: Andre Styles, eee plus, Omar Abozeid, Michael Thomson and Muthia Ashifa Salsabella on Unsplash. They show the band on more skin tones, ages and light, but only for the darker-photo case.
    • Blur, a crop and heavy JPEG compression are reported separately. They are stress tests, not ordinary selfies.
    • The docs say the display score is adjusted to look better. That is YouCam's statement, not our ground truth.
    • The cut-offs are our own heuristic. Cosmetic guidance, not medical advice.

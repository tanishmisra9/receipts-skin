The idea

Most beauty recommenders earn when you buy, so their answer is almost always yes. Receipts is built the other way around. It is allowed to say Skip, and it shows the measurement behind every verdict, so you can check the reasoning instead of trusting it.

The flow

  1. Scan. You pick a sample face or upload a selfie. YouCam Skin Analysis measures it once.
  2. Measure. You get twelve shoppable concerns, each scored from 1 to 100 (higher is healthier), with a mask showing where on the face it was measured.
  3. Verdict. Every product on the shelf gets a Match, Partial or Skip. Open any one to see its receipt, and a predicted result where YouCam Skin Simulation can render it.

Try a sample face

How a verdict is decided

Each product targets one to three skin concerns. A pore mask targets pores. For each product, Receipts takes the targeted concern that sits furthest below its cut-off and compares your score with two cut-offs.

MatchBelow 65You are weak here, and this product targets it.
Partial65 to 79A mild concern, so it may help a little.
Skip80 or moreHealthy here. You do not need this.

Hydration reads lower on every face we scanned, so it uses 50 and 65 instead. If a score is missing, the answer is Unclear, never a guess.

A worked example. On Sample C, oiliness scored 54. That is below 65, so the Matte Balance Toner is a Match. On Sample B, oiliness scored 98, so the same toner is a Skip.

There is no AI opinion in this step. It is arithmetic on your scores, and a separate script recomputes all 36 verdicts (three faces, twelve products) to check the app: scripts/recheck.py. All 36 agree.

What YouCam does

Skin AnalysisScores 14 concerns from one selfie and returns a detection mask for each. We use the raw score, not the friendlier display score, which YouCam describes as adjusted to be more favorable. One scan costs 16 units.
Skin SimulationRenders a predicted before and after for a concern such as pores or oiliness. It appears on products where YouCam can simulate that concern. One preview costs 4 units.

Your photo

The sample faces are already measured, so choosing one uploads nothing. If you scan your own face, your photo goes to the YouCam API for analysis. This site does not store it, and YouCam says it deletes uploads after 24 hours. Mask images from a live scan expire after about two hours.

For a good scan, face the camera with your head level, take your glasses off, use even light and keep your forehead clear. A scan that fails costs nothing, so retrying is safe.

What this is not

  • Not medical advice. It is cosmetic guidance. See a professional for skin conditions.
  • Not calibrated on a population. The cut-offs are our own heuristic from five faces, three of which are the samples here.
  • Sensitive to the scoring choice. Using YouCam's display score instead of the raw score would change 8 of our 36 verdicts.
  • Not a promise. A predicted result is a preview of what the model renders, not a guarantee of what a product will do.
  • Not real products. The shelf is fictional, so no brand is being rated.

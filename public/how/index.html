How it works

How a verdict is decided.

Your score against two cut-offs. Nothing else.

  1. ScanOne selfie, measured by YouCam.
  2. Measure12 concerns, each scored 1 to 100.
  3. VerdictMatch, Partial or Skip for every product.

    Good to know

    Your photo
    • Sample faces upload nothing.
    • Your own photo goes to YouCam for analysis. We store nothing, and YouCam deletes it after 24 hours.
    • Best results: face the camera, glasses off, even light.
    What this is not
    • Not medical advice.
    • Cut-offs are our own heuristic from five faces.
    • Using YouCam's display score instead of the raw score would change 8 of our 36 verdicts.
    • A predicted result is a preview, not a promise.
    • The shelf is fictional.

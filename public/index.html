How verdicts are decided

No AI opinion. Each product targets one or more skin concerns. We take the weakest targeted concern from your scan: below 65 is a Match, 65 to 79 is Partial, 80 or more is a Skip. Hydration uses 50 and 65 because the scanner reads it lower on everyone we tried. These cut-offs are our own heuristic from five faces, not clinical thresholds.

How it works
